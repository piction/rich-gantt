A · Action pill (recommended)

Click a bar body → the bar is focused: it gets a ring, unrelated tasks fade, its own dependency arrows turn accent. A compact pill floats above it with everything one click (or one key) away. Direct manipulation stays on the bar itself: drag the body to move, drag the end edge to resize (as today). Try it live in the playground.
