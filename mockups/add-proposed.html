C2 · Proposed bar as the add button

An iteration on C. Every section ends with a small proposed bar: same height and corner radius as a real bar, but light blue and dashed, and only wide enough for "+ New task". It sits where a new task would land: at the start of the chart, i.e. the earliest start of any task. The button is the preview. Clicking it turns it into the editable ghost bar, the same as "+ After".

Recommendation

Trailing row (1), with the section placeholder below the last section

  • A proposed bar reads as "the next task goes here", in the right section and on the right date, without a toolbar trip.
  • It costs one row per section. If that's too much, the header-row version (4) costs no height but reads less like a bar.
  • Keep the toolbar split button (B) as the always-visible entry point and the home for N/⇧N. The proposed bars are the in-context path.
