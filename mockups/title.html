Plan title — placement proposals

The plan gets a visible, editable title. It is the source of the download filename (Roadmap Q4 → Roadmap Q4.md) and is filled from the uploaded file name. Click to edit in place, Enter / blur commits, Esc reverts. Each proposal shows three states: rest, hover, editing. Toggle the theme top-right.

Recommendation: A, with B's meta line as an option

A gives the title the most prominent spot in the app without adding height or touching the chart pane: the title replaces the "Gantt Planner" wordmark (the app name moves to a small logo mark + the browser tab). File actions are grouped on the right, away from the title. If you want it to read like a document heading, B is the stronger "title" but costs ~45 px of chart height.
